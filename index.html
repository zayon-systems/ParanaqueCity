About This App

Parañaque Emergency Ready (PER) was created by Zayon Systems for the people of Parañaque City — because in a crisis, every second counts. We believe that access to the right information at the right moment can mean the difference between life and death.

PER puts critical emergency contacts, your personal medical profile, and life-saving guides directly in your hands — no searching, no delays. This app is our commitment to the community: to keep families safer, to help first responders act faster, and to ensure that every resident of Parañaque City is Prepared, Educated, and Ready.

Privacy & Data

Your profile, medical info, photos, and plans are stored only on your own device — PER has no servers and nothing you enter is sent anywhere unless you choose to send a message or share a photo. The app only downloads the official list of city hotline numbers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data will erase this information permanently, so keep that in mind before doing either. Some features (Windy weather map, Facebook links, Google Maps directions) open outside services when tapped; those follow their own privacy practices once you leave the app.

Disclaimer

PER is intended to facilitate quick access to emergency contacts and personal emergency information. It does not replace emergency medical services or professional advice. Always call 911 in life-threatening situations.
